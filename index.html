Privacy Policy for Tour Last updated: 17 October 2026 Tour is a free game developed by akais, Belgium. What the app collects Tour does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. What is stored on your device The game stores your best score locally on your phone. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Internet and permissions Tour does not need an internet connection and does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. Children Tour does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Changes If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
